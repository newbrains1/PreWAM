# PreWAM readability refresh

Approved in conversation on 2026-11-27. Borrow the clear hierarchy and white content surface of GAWM; preserve PreWAM blue/orange identity and scientific content.

## Approaches
Merely increasing type would leave hierarchy weak. Replacing the whole template would disturb established content. Selected: retain section order and assets, redesign typography and grouping.

## Design
White content surface on light gray outer background. Bold 30–34px section headings, ~17px desktop body, 14–15px captions. Larger authors and affiliations. Consistent paper/video buttons. Three compact contribution panels, two method explanations, distinct training stages. Keep complete benchmark tables, visibly emphasize PreWAM rows. No purple gradients or headline metric cards. Responsive mobile typography and horizontally scrollable tables.

## Constraints
Keep abstract wording, all numerical results, figures, video sources, arXiv links and citation. Preserve real-world then simulation video order. Do not edit manuscript or media assets. Keep prior source snapshot outside public site for rollback. Use existing GitHub Pages hosting.
